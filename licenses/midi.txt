MIDI module reference sources

FM envelope and waveform calculations adapted to Zig from Opal:
https://github.com/RealBitdancer/opal
The original Reality Adlib Tracker core by Shayde is public domain.

Win9x pitch and volume calculations adapted to Zig from:
https://github.com/Wohlstand/libADLMIDI/blob/master/src/models/model_win9x.c

Full Fat Man 2-op bank (embedded bank 58), unchanged WOPL data:
https://libadlmidi-js.github.io/dist/fm_banks/wopl/fatman-2op.wopl
https://github.com/Wohlstand/libADLMIDI/tree/master/fm_banks/wopl_files

The MIDI parser, event scheduler, voice manager and engine integration
are implemented in Zig for ZhuYu.
